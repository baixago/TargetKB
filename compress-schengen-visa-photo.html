Schengen Visa Photo Requirements (same across all 29 Schengen countries)

  • Size: 35mm × 45mm
  • Head height (chin to crown): 32–36mm, about 70–80% of the frame
  • Background: plain white or light grey
  • Color photo only — no black and white
  • Photo age: taken within the last 6 months
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, no glare on glasses

File Size: Check Your Application Center

Unlike the photo dimensions, the digital file size limit depends on which portal handles your visa — not just which country you're applying to. Even within a single country, the limit can differ by route:

  • VFS Global centers: commonly up to 500KB (sometimes higher)
  • Embassy or consulate direct portals: often stricter, commonly 50KB–300KB
  • Always confirm the exact number on your specific visa center's website before uploading — this tool lets you compress to any target size

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Schengen visa photo the same for Germany, Switzerland, Netherlands, Norway, and Sweden?

The photo dimensions (35×45mm) and composition rules are identical across all Schengen countries. The digital file size limit can differ depending on your visa application center, so check that separately.

What file size should my Schengen visa photo be?

It depends on your application center. VFS Global commonly asks for under 500KB, while some embassy portals require as little as 10KB–300KB. Check your specific center's instructions, then use this tool's custom size option to match it exactly.

Can I use a black and white photo for my Schengen visa?

No. Schengen visa photos must be in color.

Can I use the same photo for multiple Schengen countries?

Yes — since the 35×45mm format and biometric rules are identical across all Schengen states, one correctly-sized photo works for any of them. Just recompress to match each center's specific file size limit if needed.

My photo keeps getting rejected for file size — what should I do?

Check the exact KB limit listed on your application center's website, then use the custom size option here to compress precisely to that number rather than guessing.
