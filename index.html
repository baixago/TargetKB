GUIDE

How to Compress an Image to a Specific Size

Many online forms, government portals, exam applications, and job portals ask for a photo or document under a specific file size — often 50KB, 100KB, or 200KB. TargetKB lets you pick that exact target and get as close to it as possible without losing more quality than necessary. Upload your JPG, PNG, or WebP image, choose a preset or enter a custom size in KB, and download the result in seconds.

HOW TARGETKB WORKS

Adaptive, Browser-Based Compression

TargetKB compresses your image directly in your browser using an adaptive quality search: it tests different JPEG quality levels until it lands as close as possible to your target size, and only reduces resolution if the target can't be reached through quality adjustment alone. Because everything runs locally, your image is never uploaded to a server.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What image formats does TargetKB support?

JPG, PNG, and WebP as input. The compressed output is saved as JPEG, since JPEG compresses more predictably to a target size.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — your image is never sent to a server.

Why is my compressed image slightly under my target instead of exactly matching it?

JPEG quality doesn't map perfectly to file size. TargetKB searches for the closest possible match without going over your target.

What if my original image is already smaller than my target?

TargetKB keeps it unchanged instead of recompressing it, so you don't lose quality for no reason.

Does compression reduce image dimensions?

Only if quality reduction alone can't reach the target size. Otherwise, the original dimensions are kept.
