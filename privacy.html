PRIVACY POLICY

Your images stay in your browser

TargetKB compresses images entirely on your device using your browser's built-in Canvas API. When you select or drop an image, it is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to any server — compression, previews, and downloads all happen locally in your browser tab.

Because processing is local, we don't collect, store, or have any way to see the images you compress. We don't run analytics that track the content of your images, and we don't keep copies of anything you upload or download.

This site may use standard, privacy-respecting hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) which can log basic technical information like IP address and browser type for security and performance purposes, the same way most websites do. This is separate from and unrelated to your image content.

This policy may be updated from time to time as TargetKB changes. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
