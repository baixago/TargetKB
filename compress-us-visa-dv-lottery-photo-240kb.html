US Visa & DV Lottery Photo Requirements at a Glance

  • File format: JPEG only (no PNG, HEIC, or PDF)
  • File size: 240KB or smaller
  • Dimensions: Typically square, roughly 600×600 to 1200×1200 pixels (can vary slightly by form/category — verify for yours)
  • Background: Plain white or off-white

Common Reasons US Visa Photos Get Rejected

  • File is in PNG or HEIC format instead of JPEG
  • File size is over 240KB (very common straight out of a modern phone camera)
  • Photo isn't square, or falls outside the 600×600–1200×1200 pixel range
  • Background isn't plain white

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DS-160 photo size requirement?

The DS-160, DS-260, and DV Lottery photo must be a JPEG file of 240KB or less, roughly square in the 600×600 to 1200×1200 pixel range, with a plain white or off-white background.

Can I use my US passport photo for my DV Lottery application?

The composition rules (background, head size, expression) are the same, but check the file itself — passport renewal allows up to 10MB, while DV Lottery requires 240KB or smaller. You'll likely need to compress it first.

Why do I need to compress my photo to 240KB or less?

Photos over 240KB are generally not accepted by the online application systems, regardless of how good the photo looks. Getting under the limit is a technical requirement, separate from photo quality.

My phone saves photos as HEIC — will that work?

Not directly — TargetKB accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP. If your phone saved a HEIC file, switch your camera to JPG format first (most phones have this option in settings) or convert the file to JPG, then upload it here. The tool's output is always JPEG, which is what DS-160 and DV Lottery require.

Will compressing reduce my photo's quality too much?

The tool adjusts JPEG compression to help bring the file below your selected target size while retaining as much image quality as possible.
